Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Roberto is a private assistant used by a single family. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Google user data

Roberto requests access to Google Calendar events (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events) so it can create events on the family's calendar when a family member asks it to. It does not read, sell, or share any other Google account data.

What is stored

  • The Google authorization token, stored on the family's own server and readable only by the assistant.
  • The shared grocery list and checklists, stored on the same server.
  • Recent chat messages, kept in memory only and cleared whenever the assistant restarts.

How messages are processed

To understand requests, messages are processed by a language model. That model either runs locally on the family's server, or is Google's Gemini API, in which case messages are sent to Google under Google's Gemini API terms. Messages are delivered through Telegram under Telegram's privacy policy.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke Roberto's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
